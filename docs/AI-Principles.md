# WorkCore / Koll – AI Principles

## Core Principle
> WorkCore thinks with the user, but the human decides.

## AI Purpose
AI should:
- reduce friction
- save time
- improve quality
- detect risks
- suggest next steps
- structure unstructured input
- automate routine work when approved

## Proactive Help
AI may proactively suggest useful actions when there is a clear benefit.

## Human Approval
AI must never perform critical or irreversible business actions without explicit user confirmation.

Examples:
- sending invoices
- cancelling jobs
- deleting data
- recording payments
- contacting customers
- moving important appointments

## Transparency
AI involvement should be visible when useful, without over-labeling everything.

## Explainability
Important recommendations and warnings must be explainable on demand.

## Optionality
WorkCore must remain fully usable without AI.

## Company-Level AI Settings
Companies should be able to configure the level of AI assistance.

## Human Review
Important AI-generated content should be reviewable before use.

## Learning from Use
AI should improve suggestions based on repeated workflows, while preserving user control.

## Language and Tone
AI should adapt output to:
- language
- country
- context
- tone

Initial languages:
- Swedish
- German
- English

## Automatic Language Detection
Infer the appropriate language where practical, but allow override.

## Translation
Provide in-app translation.

## Summarization
AI should summarize long:
- communication threads
- project histories
- service histories
- reports
- notes

Original content must remain accessible.

## Speech to Structured Data
Users should be able to dictate natural language and let WorkCore extract:
- customer
- task
- duration
- material
- report text

## Camera + AI
AI may interpret photos for:
- receipts
- odometer readings
- damage descriptions
- document data

Results must remain reviewable.

## Document Understanding
AI should extract structured data from:
- PDFs
- invoices
- delivery notes
- forms

## Tasks and Dates from Communication
AI should recognize possible:
- appointments
- deadlines
- callbacks
- service intervals
- tasks

and suggest structured entries.

## Open Item Detection
AI should identify unresolved actions from jobs, notes, reports and communication.

## Next-Step Suggestions
AI should propose sensible next actions without forcing a workflow.

## Risk Detection
AI should flag emerging operational risks.

## Economic Anomaly Detection
AI should help identify unusual:
- profitability
- labor time
- material cost
- payment behavior

Important: explain the basis.

## Improvement Suggestions
AI should suggest workflow improvements.

## Automation
Automations must be:
- visible
- understandable
- pausable
- editable
- disableable

New automations should be suggested first, not silently activated.

## Templates
AI should detect repeated patterns and suggest useful templates.

## Data Minimization
AI should generally use only data relevant to the current task.

## Final AI Rule
> AI may reduce work. It must never reduce trust.
