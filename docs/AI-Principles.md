# AI Principles

## Core principle
> WorkCore thinks along — but does not decide for the user.

AI may help with drafting, summaries, open items, priorities, next steps, speech-to-structured-data, document/photo extraction, date/task recognition, anomaly detection, risk detection, workflow suggestions, translation, company knowledge and action preparation.

### Rules
- Use only necessary and permitted context.
- Respect tenant and role boundaries.
- Important actions remain reviewable.
- Explain what was generated and what is uncertain.
- Cite/link internal sources where practical.
- Distinguish suggestions from confirmed facts.
- Learn work patterns carefully and controllably.
- Propose automations; do not silently activate significant ones.
- Core product workflows must work without AI.
