# Modules and Capabilities

Long-term capability map; not implementation order.

## Core platform
Company/tenant management, users, roles, localization, settings, audit history, notifications, documents/media, search, reporting, automation, AI, integrations, import/export.

## CRM
Customers, contacts, locations, leads, inquiries, timeline, communication history, contracts, customer-specific terms.

## Jobs / orders
One-off and recurring jobs, assignments, planning, status, materials, services, checklists, reports, completion and billing handoff.

## Projects / objects / sites
Configurable types:
- Object
- Project
- Construction site
- Location
- Facility
- Other

Existing data without type defaults to Object.

## Planning/resources
Employees, teams, vehicles, equipment, tools, capacity, absences, qualifications, conflict detection and resource assignment.

## Time
Time tracking, job time, corrections, approvals, absence and reporting.

## Vehicles / drive log
Vehicles, mileage, trips, one active trip per vehicle, offline support, evidence, corrections with reason and history.

## Inventory/purchasing
Items, services, consumables, assets, stock, suppliers, purchasing, barcode/QR and mobile scanning.

## Offers/billing
Offers, price lists, invoices, recurring billing, payment status, reminders/dunning and service evidence.

## Communication/documents
Internal/external messages, email, shared files, PDFs, photos, signatures, versioning, retention.

## Service quality
Service reports, acceptance, defects, complaints, QA, templates and evidence.

## Tasks/calendar
Tasks, due dates, recurring work, escalation, handover, appointments, resources and reminders.

## Portals/self-service
Customer portal and employee self-service.

## Analytics
Operational dashboards, profitability, post-calculation, utilization, custom reports and drill-down.

## Knowledge
Procedures, lessons learned, customer/project knowledge and AI-searchable internal knowledge.

## Multi-company
Separate legal settings, numbering, tax, reporting and permissions with optional shared master data.

## Extensibility
API, webhooks, events, adapters, configurable fields/forms/workflows and industry profiles.
