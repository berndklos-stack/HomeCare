# Product Strategy

## Positioning
A simple but professional business operating platform for SMEs.

Compete on:
- lower friction
- integrated workflows
- reliability
- faster daily work
- mobile usability
- clear pricing
- modular fit

## Market focus
First: Sweden and Germany.
Later: Austria, Switzerland and other European markets with proper localization.

## Priority industries
Highest:
- crafts/service
- property services/property operations

Strong adjacent:
- consulting/office
- agriculture

Lower priority:
- logistics-related service
- other operational SMEs

## Pricing principles
- simple pricing
- low entry barrier
- monthly cancellation
- company package + user scaling
- avoid excessive fragmentation
- core functionality not artificially crippled
- transparent variable/usage costs
- fair upgrades/downgrades
- no data loss on downgrade
- free trial, preferably no card
- annual discount may be offered
- market-specific pricing may be appropriate

## Sales/support
Self-service purchase for smaller customers; personal consulting, paid setup and migration help for larger/complex customers.

## Growth
Growth should primarily come from a product customers recommend because it materially improves daily work.

## Brand
Shared design/product system across WorkCore and Koll. The brand should remain strong independent of the founder.
